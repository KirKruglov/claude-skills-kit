# Data Room Prep Checklist

Get your fundraising documents investor-ready with a stage-specific due-diligence checklist, folder structure, and gap analysis — no SaaS required.

---

## Overview

Data Room Prep Checklist generates a targeted preparation package for founders entering seed or Series A due diligence. From your round stage and business model, it produces a prioritized checklist across six categories (Legal, Financials, Product, Team, Commercial, Cap Table), a copy-paste-ready folder structure for Google Drive or Notion, and a concrete gap list showing what you're likely missing and what investors will ask next. Use this skill when you've received a term sheet and need to organize your documents, when you're preparing for a first VC meeting, or when you want to audit your current data room before a formal process begins.

---

## Requirements

- Your round stage (pre-seed / seed / Series A / Series B+)
- Your business model type (SaaS / marketplace / hardware / services / other)
- Optional: investor type (VC / angel / strategic / family office)
- Optional: your existing data room contents pasted as a list — triggers gap analysis mode

No external tools, CRM, or integrations required.

---

## How to Use

1. **Identify your round context**
   - Know your current stage (or approximate it) and your business model
   - Optionally, copy a list of documents you already have in your data room

2. **Trigger the skill by saying:**
   - "Data room checklist for my seed round"
   - "Prepare my data room for Series A"
   - Or in Russian: "Чек-лист дата рума для seed раунда" or "Подготовка data room"

3. **Provide your context**
   - State your stage and business model in a single message
   - Optionally paste your existing document list for a gap analysis

4. **Review your package**
   - Get a checklist with Must-have / Expected / Nice-to-have priority labels
   - Get a folder structure to set up immediately in Google Drive or Notion
   - Get a gap list with the most likely missing items and predictable investor follow-up questions

## Examples

### Example 1: Seed Round Preparation (SaaS)

**Input:**
```
Preparing for seed round, B2B SaaS, raising from VCs.
```

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
# Data Room Checklist — Seed Round · B2B SaaS
> For: VC / institutional

## Due Diligence Checklist

### Legal & Corporate
- [ ] **[Must-have]** Certificate of Incorporation / Articles of Association
- [ ] **[Must-have]** Cap table (fully diluted, current)
- [ ] **[Expected]** Shareholders agreement
- [ ] **[Expected]** IP assignment agreements (all founders)
- [ ] **[Nice-to-have]** Option pool / ESOP schedule

### Financials
- [ ] **[Must-have]** 3-year financial model (P&L, cash flow, balance sheet)
- [ ] **[Must-have]** MRR breakdown — last 12 months (monthly)
- [ ] **[Expected]** Bank statements — last 3–6 months
...

## Gap List

### What You're Likely Missing (Seed)
1. Signed IP assignment agreements from all founders
2. Board consent resolutions for prior equity grants
3. Reference customer list with contact permission

### What the Investor Will Ask Next
1. "Show me monthly MRR for the last 12 months"
2. "Who are your 3 reference customers and can we speak with them?"

**Next step:** Send this folder structure to your lawyer and confirm all legal documents are countersigned originals, not drafts.
```

### Example 2: Existing Data Room Gap Analysis

**Input:**
```
Series A round, marketplace. Here's what I have so far:
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Cap table (Excel)
- 2-year financial model
- Team bios
- Pitch deck
```

**Output (excerpt):**
```markdown
# Data Room Checklist — Series A · Marketplace
> Gap analysis mode — comparing your list against Series A requirements

## Checklist — Delta View

### Legal & Corporate
- ✓ Certificate of Incorporation
- ✓ Cap table (confirm it's fully diluted with post-money projection)
- ✗ Shareholders agreement — missing
- ✗ IP assignment agreements (all founders) — missing
- ✗ Board consent resolutions for equity grants — missing

### Financials
- ⚠ 2-year financial model — present but Series A expects 3-year with unit economics
- ✗ MRR breakdown (monthly, last 12 months) — missing
- ✗ Bank statements (last 3–6 months) — missing
...

### What the Investor Will Ask Next
1. "Can you share your cohort retention data?"
2. "What's your current GMV and take rate by month?"
```
